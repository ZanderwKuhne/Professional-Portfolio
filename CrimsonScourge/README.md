***********************
Crimson Scurge
***********************

Crimson Scurge is a CLI textbase adventure game set in a fantasy world
terrorised by a Vampire overlord. You will choose one of 3 starting characters
and embark on a personal quest to right the wrongs of the world. 

Gather a party of up to 4 adventurers, complete quests in the world, overcome dangerous
enemy encounters. Your decisions matter
